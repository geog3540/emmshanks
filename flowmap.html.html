Flowmap

Areas that receive the most concentrated flows are central/South of United States of America. States with the most incoming number of flows includes Texas, Oklahoma, and Colorado. States with the most outgoing number of flows includes Nebraska, Iowa, Missouri, Illinois, Kansas, Alabama, Tennessee, and Kentucky. 2. Generally, the most activity for flows is in the Midwest/South area. The flow lines for these areas are most densely concentrated there, with most people moving to the Southern direction. The flows thickness indicates that a lot of family migrations happen in these areas. Some people are moving west as well, but generally they are moving away from the Eastern/Central area. 3. Generally, the node symbols follow a lot of the choropleth map background with some outliers like Idaho and Colorado. There are a couple of flow lines that cause confusion, such as the thick flow line between Iowa and Nebraska, despite there being low populations for both. But generally, the different datasets tell a cohesive story about family movement patterns.
